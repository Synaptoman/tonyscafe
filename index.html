Our Story

BUILT FOR THE SIDE-HUSTLERS

Tony was a man who could fix anything, build anything, and never turned down a challenge. A Port Elizabeth garage, a stack of dog-eared Bike SA magazines, and the conviction that if something was worth doing, it was worth doing properly.

Tony's Cafe started as a motorcycle workshop. It grew into something bigger — a platform for real skills and real service, built around the same values Tony lived by: show up, do the work, take pride in the result.

We're not a corporate. Not a franchise. We're a collection of side-hustles that grew up — solar cleaning, web design, home repairs, and more to come. Each service is hands-on, local, and personal.

We started in Port Elizabeth. We're growing across South Africa. And we're just getting started.
