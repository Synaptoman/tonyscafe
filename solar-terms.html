1. About This Service

2. Scope of Service

Our service includes the external cleaning of solar photovoltaic panels using deionised water and industry-standard soft-bristle cleaning equipment. Our service does not include:

  • Inspection, testing or electrical assessment of the solar system
  • Cleaning of inverters, wiring, mounting hardware or any component other than the panel glass surface
  • Repair of any pre-existing damage, cracks, delamination or defects
  • Internal panel cleaning or any process requiring panel removal
  • Roof repairs, waterproofing or structural work

3. Client Responsibilities & Pre-Existing Conditions

The client accepts full responsibility for:

  • Ensuring that all solar panels, mounting systems and roof structures are in sound condition prior to the cleaning service
  • Disclosing any known defects, cracks, loose mountings, leaks or damage before the service is performed
  • Ensuring that access to the property and panels is safe and legally permitted
  • Ensuring that the solar system is switched off or in a safe state for cleaning as required by the inverter manufacturer
  • Providing access to a water tap within a reasonable distance of the panels

Tony's Cafe Solar Cleaning reserves the right to refuse or stop any cleaning job where conditions are deemed unsafe or where pre-existing damage is observed that could be worsened by cleaning. In such cases a call-out fee may apply.
