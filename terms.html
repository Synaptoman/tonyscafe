2. Scope of Service

Tony's Cafe Racers operates on a labour-only model. This means:

  • All quoted amounts cover labour only — the skill, time and expertise of our team
  • All parts, components, materials and consumables required for the build are supplied by the client unless explicitly stated otherwise in writing
  • We do not mark up or resell parts. Clients source their own parts at their own cost
  • Where specialist sub-contracted work is required (e.g. chrome plating, upholstery, precision machining), we will coordinate and manage the sub-contractor on the client's behalf. Sub-contractor costs are invoiced separately and are not included in the labour quote
  • All quality control, transport coordination, job specifications and client communication are handled in-house by Tony's Cafe Racers

What is included in every build: Strip and assessment, specified labour tasks as per the accepted quote, reassembly, final setup and quality check, and all job management and logistics coordination.

The specific scope of work for each build is set out in the written quote issued to the client. No work outside the agreed scope will be undertaken without a revised written quote and client approval.

3. Quotes & Acceptance

  • All quotes are provided in writing via email and are valid for 30 days from the date of issue
  • Quotes are based on the information provided by the client and a visual inspection of the donor motorcycle. Additional work identified during the build that falls outside the original scope will be quoted separately
  • A quote is accepted and a build slot confirmed only upon receipt of the required deposit payment
  • Verbal agreements do not constitute acceptance. Written confirmation and payment are required

4. Payment Terms

The following payment structure applies to all builds:

  • Deposit: A deposit of 50% of the total quoted labour amount (or such other amount as agreed in writing) is required to confirm your build slot. Work will not commence until the deposit has cleared
  • Balance: The remaining balance is due in full upon completion of the build, before the motorcycle is released for collection or delivery
  • Full payment upfront: Clients may elect to pay the full quoted amount in advance. This may be offered a priority position in the build queue at our discretion
  • Additional work: Any additional work approved during the build is invoiced separately and is due upon completion

Payments are processed securely via PayFast, South Africa's leading payment gateway. Tony's Cafe Racers never stores your card details. Accepted payment methods include credit card, debit card and EFT.

All amounts are quoted and invoiced in South African Rand (ZAR). Prices are exclusive of VAT. Tony's Cafe Racers is not currently registered as a VAT vendor.

7. Refunds & Cancellation Policy

We understand that circumstances change. Our cancellation and refund policy is as follows:

  • Cancellation before work commences: If the client cancels after paying a deposit but before any work has begun, a cancellation fee of 20% of the deposit will be retained to cover administrative costs. The balance of the deposit will be refunded within 14 business days
